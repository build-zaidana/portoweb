A menu lists what the kitchen can make and what you need to say to get it. You never walk into the kitchen. An API works the same way between two programs.

## The menu is the contract

It says which dishes exist, what you can change, and what comes back.

## The waiter is the request

You ask in a fixed format. The waiter brings back a dish or a clear "we're out of that".
